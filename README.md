# 💰 Nudge

**Your money. Your decisions. A smarter nudge.**

Nudge is a smart personal money-management application designed to help users **track, understand, and control their spending**.

> **Don't just track your money. Understand it.**

---

## 🎯 Vision

Nudge aims to act like a **financial friend** — helping users make better spending decisions instead of simply recording transactions.

Traditional expense trackers tell you **where your money went**.

Nudge aims to help you understand **why you're spending it** and eventually **whether you should spend it**.

---

## 🚀 Core Features

* 💰 Track income and expenses
* 📊 Monitor account balance
* 🔄 See balance before and after spending
* 🏷️ Categorize transactions
* 👥 Track money lent to friends
* 🤝 Track money borrowed
* 📈 Monitor spending patterns
* 🔔 Provide intelligent spending nudges
* 🧠 Help users make smarter spending decisions
* 🤖 AI-powered financial guidance *(planned)*

## 💡 Product Philosophy

Nudge isn't meant to be another expense spreadsheet.

It's designed around three questions:

**Where did my money go?**

**Why am I spending this much?**

**Should I spend this money right now?**

That's the difference between **tracking money** and **understanding money**.
